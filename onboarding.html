Stepture is installed 🎉

Capture annotated screenshots while you test, then export a clean Word test log — grouped by scenario.

📌 Pin it for one-click access

1
Click the 🧩 puzzle-piece icon at the top-right of Chrome.
2
Find Stepture in the list.
3
Click the pin icon next to it so it stays on your toolbar.

How to use it

  1. Click the icon and Start Session — enter a document title, tester, environment, and your first scenario.
  2. On the page, the toolbar appears. Toggle Annotate: On, then add boxes, arrows, highlights, text labels, or redactions, and hit Capture.
  3. Add a description, mark Pass/Fail (Fail adds Expected/Actual), and the step is saved.
  4. Finished a scenario? Click ＋ Scenario to start the next one — steps restart at 1.
  5. Open the popup and Export Word Log to download the .docx.
Tip: while Annotate is on, the overlay catches clicks — toggle it off to scroll or click the page, then back on to draw.
